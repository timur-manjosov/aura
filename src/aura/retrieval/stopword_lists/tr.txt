# exactly like a question: lower case, and dotless "ı" folded to "i" like every
# other Latin diacritic, so "nasıl", "nasil" and "NASIL" are the same word.

# "time", a subject in its own right). Kept although they are first names in

# questions to leave to IDF. See aura.retrieval.stopwords.
